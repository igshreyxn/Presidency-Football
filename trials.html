How Trials Work

1

Show Up & Warm Up

Arrive 15 minutes early. Wear comfortable sportswear and bring water. Boots are helpful but not required for your first session.

2

Small-Sided Games

You'll play in small groups so coaches can see how you move, pass, and react — not a high-pressure one-on-one evaluation.

3

Hear Back Within a Week

Whether or not it's a fit this round, you'll get a direct response — and an invite to future open sessions either way.
